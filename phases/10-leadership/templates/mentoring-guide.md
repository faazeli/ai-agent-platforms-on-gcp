# Mentoring through Phase 1: a four-week plan

**Mentee:** · **Goal they chose (their words):** · **Meeting:** weekly, 45 minutes

Your success metric is *their* pull request merged, not yours.

## The GROW model, one question set per meeting

- **Goal:** What do you want from this session? What would make this week a success?
- **Reality:** What have you tried? What happened? What's in the way?
- **Options:** What could you do? What else? What would you do if you weren't stuck on X?
- **Way forward:** What will you do before next time? How will you know it worked? What support do you need?

Ask more than you tell. Count it: aim for at least three questions for every piece of advice.

## Week by week

| Week | Their focus | Your focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lab 1.1–1.2: Pydantic models and settings | Agree on the goal; pair for 20 minutes, they drive |
| 2 | Lab 1.3: endpoints and the repository | Review their first PR together using the code review guide |
| 3 | Lab 1.4: Problem Details | Let them get stuck for a while; ask, don't fix |
| 4 | Lab 1.5 + their errors.md | They demo to you (5 minutes); you give one strength and one growth area |
