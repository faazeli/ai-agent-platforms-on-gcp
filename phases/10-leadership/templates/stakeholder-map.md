# Influence without authority: stakeholder map

Place each person by how much power they have over the decision and how much they care about it.

| | Low interest | High interest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High power** | **Keep satisfied.** Short updates; no surprises. | **Manage closely.** Pre-wire 1:1 before any group meeting. |
| **Low power** | **Monitor.** | **Keep informed.** Invite feedback; they often become your champions. |

## Pre-wiring rules
- Nobody should hear your proposal for the first time in the meeting where it's decided.
- Ask for objections, not approval: "What would make you say no?"
- Change the proposal when an objection is right, and tell that person you did.
